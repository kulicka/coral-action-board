Coral MSP action board · design study 02

One card, three layouts

Pick the layout that suits the job. Short cards and ledger rows unfold in place to show the full detail, so nobody has to leave the board to read a task. Every layout uses the same symbols for type, source, priority, owner and SLA. The people and tickets are made up.

The visual language

Each attribute has its own channel. Type is a shape plus a hue, priority is signal bars, and only the SLA clock uses green, amber and red. Ownership goes in the avatar slot.

Parked for later

Time tracking on the card. Show logged against estimated hours. Taking a task would start a timer, and Stop would post the time to the time tracking system.
